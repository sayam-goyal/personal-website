A personal website can be a notebook with a front door. Somewhere for unfinished questions, useful discoveries, and projects that deserve more context than a repository description.

## A place to connect the dots

Computer science has a way of making one question turn into five. An experiment with language models can lead to evaluation, statistics, systems, or an entirely different problem.

This space is designed for those connections: an explanation that finally clicked, a result that was surprising, or a small thing worth sharing.

> A useful note doesn’t need to be the final word on a topic. It just needs to make one thing clearer.

## Small is a good starting point

The format can stay simple:

- What question started the experiment?
- What did you try?
- What happened, and what would you do differently?

Code belongs here too:

```python
def keep_exploring(question):
    experiment = try_something_small(question)
    return experiment.lessons
```

The goal is a place that grows with the work. One note at a time.
